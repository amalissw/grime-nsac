How it sounds

Earth Jukebox turns satellite data into music. Here's how every sound maps to the data.

Honest note: The sound is built from pixel colors in NASA visualization images, matched against the layer's color legend. This is an interpretation of the visualization, not a calibrated measurement. The same data shown with a different color scale would produce different sounds.
